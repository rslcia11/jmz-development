# Copy — voice and rules

How JMZ writes on the site. Every new sentence should pass these checks.

## Positioning in one line

JMZ builds ERP systems, AI models and websites — and understands the problem
before writing code.

## Principles (and why)

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** A visitor must know what JMZ builds within
   five seconds. B2B visitors who understand the offer that fast convert 2–3×
   more; clever or abstract headlines lose.
   ([Apexure](https://www.apexure.com/blog/landing-page-conversion-rate-optimisation-tips-clarity-is-everything/),
   [Heyflow](https://heyflow.com/blog/b2b-landing-page-best-practices/))
2. **Objective, not promotional.** No marketese. In NN/g's study, objective
   copy measured 27% better usability; concise +58%; concise + scannable +
   objective +124%.
   ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/))
3. **Specific beats generic.** Name deliverables (ERP, AI models, websites)
   and concrete behaviours, not adjectives. Generic positioning is why most dev
   agency sites read the same.
   ([100signals](https://100signals.com/best-positioning-agencies-for-software-development-companies/),
   [April Dunford](https://businessofsoftware.org/talks/speakers/april-dunford/))
4. **Commitments instead of claims.** "Las malas noticias llegan temprano"
   says more than "somos transparentes".
5. **True or out.** No invented clients, metrics, testimonials or awards
   (master plan §30, §76). Project copy in `src/data/projects.ts` is a draft
   until each detail is confirmed against the real project.

## Voice

- Spanish, neutral Latin American, *tuteo* ("tu negocio", "te lo diremos").
- First person plural for JMZ ("construimos", "entendemos").
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Technical mono labels (eyebrows, categories) stay in English, as a design
  convention; headings and body are Spanish.

## Banned phrases

"soluciones innovadoras", "transformamos tu negocio", "llevamos tu empresa al
siguiente nivel", "de vanguardia", "líderes en", "pasión por", "360°",
"sinergia", "tecnología de punta", "a la medida de tus sueños" — and any
sentence that would fit any other agency's site unchanged.

## Headline test

Before shipping a heading, ask: could a competitor publish this exact sentence?
If yes, rewrite it until only JMZ could say it truthfully.
